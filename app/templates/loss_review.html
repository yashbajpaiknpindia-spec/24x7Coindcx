HOW TO READ IT

What counts as a potentially wrong exit?

A horizon is labelled “potentially exited too early” only when the modeled after-tax endpoint would exceed the actual after-tax result by more than a small tolerance. It does not mean the position never hit a stop or liquidation price between samples. “Exit better than holding” means the sampled endpoint is worse than the actual exit, not that every stop was perfectly timed.

Older trade records have incomplete entry-time snapshots. The review distinguishes missing data from an avoided loss. 24-hour outcomes remain pending until sufficient post-exit history exists. Modeled tax reserves are indicative, not a tax return; 30% VDA tax and 1% transfer TDS are based on Income Tax Department guidance, but specific futures/derivative treatment and eligibility/exemptions require professional verification.
