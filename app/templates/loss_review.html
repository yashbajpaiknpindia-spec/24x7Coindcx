HOW TO READ IT

What counts as a potentially wrong exit?

A horizon is labelled “potentially exited too early” only when the modeled after-tax endpoint would exceed the actual after-tax result by more than a small tolerance. It does not mean the position never hit a stop or liquidation price between samples. “Exit better than holding” means the sampled endpoint is worse than the actual exit, not that every stop was perfectly timed.

Older trade records have incomplete entry-time snapshots. The page reuses stored five-minute quotes to calculate endpoint results plus the best/worst sampled executable-side P&L after the exit. Longer horizons remain pending until enough post-exit observations accumulate. A positive peak is an ex-post possibility, not proof a chosen exit rule could have captured it or that a position would have survived drawdown. Modeled tax reserves are indicative, not a tax return; Spot's default planning reserve is 31.2% (30% tax plus 4% cess) before any surcharge; 1% transfer TDS is shown separately and is not an extra tax. Global Futures use your individual income-tax slab, so the editable rate is only an estimate. Confirm applicability and treatment with a tax professional.
