Current simulation assumptions

Cost model adds 18% GST on the trading fee (not on notional); modeled fee incl. GST is {{'%.4f%%'|format(cfg.get('scalper_spot_fee_pct',0.15)*1.18)}}% spot and {{'%.4f%%'|format(cfg.get('scalper_futures_fee_pct',0.075)*1.18)}}% futures. Normal slippage {{cfg.get('scalper_normal_slippage_pct',0.05)}}% or stress {{cfg.get('scalper_stress_slippage_pct',0.12)}}%. Round-trip fees/slippage are calculated on entry notional plus exit notional, and bid/ask execution already includes the spread.

Spot tax reserve {{cfg.get('scalper_spot_tax_pct',31.2)}}% is a planning reserve on positive gross VDA gains (30% plus 4% cess before surcharge). Futures reserve {{cfg.get('scalper_futures_tax_pct',31.2)}}% is an editable slab estimate, not a universal tax rate. Spot TDS is display-only and not deducted twice. CoinDCX’s own help pages describe user-tier fees and per-side charging; actual account fees must be confirmed under Fee Details.
