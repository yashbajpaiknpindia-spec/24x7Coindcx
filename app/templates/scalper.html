Fast scalping lab

Independent paper-only momentum scalper for liquid CoinDCX spot and futures. All time displays use IST; no exchange orders are sent.

Execution truth: the worker refreshes REST market snapshots about every 20 seconds when each cycle completes. Signal confirmation uses stored approximately 5-minute observations plus a 10-minute direction check. This is a fast paper simulation, not tick-level HFT or a guarantee of a real fill. Exits are checked every worker cycle; if data is stale, positions are not falsely marked as filled.
Profit-factor target is 1.25, not a prediction. PF can only be measured after trades close. The app labels the target as unvalidated until at least 50 closed paper trades exist; entries must still pass liquidity, spread, momentum agreement and estimated cost gates. Current trade history is not evidence of a proven PF yet.

COST & TAX POLICY

What “net after tax” means in this simulation

Entry and exit are priced at the current executable side of the book; modeled taker fees and slippage are charged on both sides. A configurable 30% reserve is applied to positive gross gains before surcharge/cess to avoid pretending trading expenses automatically reduce the VDA tax base. For spot, an illustrative 1% TDS amount is shown separately as possible cash withholding, not subtracted again from profit because it may be creditable. TDS thresholds and tax treatment—especially for futures/derivatives—are not determined by this paper model; confirm your classification and filing with a tax professional.

This mode defaults to one-times simulated exposure, at most three positions and a 15-minute time exit. It is research—not a claim that >1.25 profit factor is achievable. Live orders remain disabled.
