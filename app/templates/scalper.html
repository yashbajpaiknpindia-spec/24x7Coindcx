Fast scalping lab

Two isolated paper tracks: a wider research track and a capital-constrained track. Neither sends exchange orders.

Review losses & missed signals →
Execution truth: the worker refreshes REST market snapshots about every 20 seconds when each cycle completes. Signal confirmation uses stored approximately 5-minute observations plus a 10-minute direction check. This is a fast paper simulation, not tick-level HFT or a guarantee of a real fill. Exits are checked every worker cycle; if data is stale, positions are not falsely marked as filled.
Important: 5m / 10m returns are backward-looking, not a forecast. The system estimates post-cost viability and displays order-book depth, but only forward paper outcomes can show whether the signal works. Open “Review losses & missed signals” to compare accepted losers with rejected candidates.

COST & TAX POLICY

What “net after tax” means in this simulation

Entries and exits use executable bid/ask quotes. The spread is therefore reflected in simulated gross P&L and is not deducted again. Taker fees and slippage are separate and charged on both sides. Model: spot fee {{cfg.get('scalper_spot_fee_pct',0.15)}}%/side, futures fee {{cfg.get('scalper_futures_fee_pct',0.075)}}%/side, normal slippage {{cfg.get('scalper_normal_slippage_pct',0.05)}}%/side or stress {{cfg.get('scalper_stress_slippage_pct',0.12)}}%/side. These are editable assumptions, not verified account-level fees. Spot tax reserve is {{cfg.get('scalper_spot_tax_pct',31.2)}}% on positive gross VDA transfer gains; futures uses an editable {{cfg.get('scalper_futures_tax_pct',31.2)}}% slab/cess planning reserve. 1% spot TDS is display-only and never deducted a second time; applicability depends on payer and thresholds. This is not tax advice.

This mode defaults to one-times simulated exposure, at most three positions and a 15-minute time exit. It is research—not a claim that >1.25 profit factor is achievable. Live orders remain disabled.
