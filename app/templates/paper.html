Paper trading

Live CoinDCX market data scan and paper simulation only; no exchange orders are created. Research mode evaluates signals independently of configured account capital.

Two independent modes run on each valid cycle: Unrestricted research uses fixed USDT notional and ignores account capital. Capital-constrained mode reserves spot cash/futures margin against starting capital and uses a separate trade/position ledger. Both are paper-only; no exchange orders are sent.
Cycle behavior: Start queues an immediate paper cycle even if today’s daily run already happened. After that, automatic rebalances run once per UTC day; “Run cycle now” forces an extra paper-only evaluation. A separate background worker must be online for queued automatic runs. No real exchange orders are sent in paper mode.
Risk controls: Paper positions use a 0.75% adverse price-move hard stop, plus an earlier exit after three consecutive fresh checks at or beyond -0.35%. Entries require 24h and 72h trend agreement and an estimated move large enough to clear modeled round-trip fees, spread and slippage. Stops use the executable side of the current order book; this remains a simulation, not a guaranteed fill.
Live data only: no synthetic/mock prices are used. If API refresh fails or data is stale, the engine should skip signals rather than invent prices. Liquidation prices are rough estimates only; this version cannot certify past liquidation without historical intrabar candles and exchange-specific maintenance-margin tiers.

SIGNAL DECISION AUDIT

Rejected trades: causes & counterfactual tracking

Every shortlisted candidate is recorded with the gate that accepted or rejected it. Future executable quotes are checked at 1h, 4h and 24h to estimate whether rejecting it avoided a loss or missed a winner. These are shadow outcomes, not real fills. Summary counts use the latest 10,000 decisions; records are retained for up to 90 days and capped at 100,000 rows.
