Paper trading

Live CoinDCX market data scan and paper simulation only; no exchange orders are created. Research mode evaluates signals independently of configured account capital.

Cycle behavior: Start queues an immediate paper cycle even if today’s daily run already happened. After that, automatic rebalances run once per UTC day; “Run cycle now” forces an extra paper-only evaluation. A separate background worker must be online for queued automatic runs. No real exchange orders are sent in paper mode.
Live data only: no synthetic/mock prices are used. If API refresh fails or data is stale, the engine should skip signals rather than invent prices. Liquidation prices are rough estimates only; this version cannot certify past liquidation without historical intrabar candles and exchange-specific maintenance-margin tiers.
